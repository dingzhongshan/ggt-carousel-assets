Bloated after a meal and not sure why? Sometimes the way we eat plays a part.

The National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases explains that everyone swallows a little air when eating and drinking, and that eating or drinking too fast can mean swallowing more, which can lead to more gas. Doctors may recommend eating slowly, sitting down to eat, and trying smaller, more frequent meals. They may also suggest limiting gum, hard candy, fizzy drinks and straws.

Small, gentle changes, one at a time. If bloating keeps bothering you, a doctor can help.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
