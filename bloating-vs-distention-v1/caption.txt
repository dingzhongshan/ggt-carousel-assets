Bloated isn't always the same as bigger.

NIDDK explains that bloating is a feeling of fullness or swelling in your belly, while distention is when your belly actually becomes larger than usual. Only about half of people with bloating also have distention, so how it feels and how it looks can differ.

Research suggests 16% to 31% of people experience bloating, and some gas symptoms are normal, especially around meals. If yours bother you, change suddenly, or come with belly pain, constipation, diarrhea or weight loss, it's worth talking with your doctor.
