Colonoscopy coming up, and feeling uneasy? Knowing the steps can help.

NIDDK explains that a doctor uses a flexible tube with a camera to look inside your colon. It usually takes less than an hour.

Prep happens at home with written instructions, and you may need a clear-liquid diet for a day or more. The prep causes diarrhea, so stay close to a bathroom. Tell your doctor about all your medicines, vitamins and supplements, and line up a ride home.

Sedation helps you not be aware or feel pain. Afterward, you may feel cramping or bloating for the first hour, and you can expect to be back to your normal diet by the next day. Get care right away for severe belly pain, fever, or bleeding that doesn't stop.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
