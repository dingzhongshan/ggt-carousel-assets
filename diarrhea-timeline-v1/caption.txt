Still not back to normal after a stomach bug? Here's a quick timeline.

NIDDK says acute diarrhea typically lasts less than a week and goes away on its own. Lasting longer than 2 weeks is called persistent, and at least 4 weeks is chronic. After an infection, you may have trouble digesting certain carbs, which can keep things loose longer.

Adults should talk with a doctor if it lasts more than 2 days. Frequent vomiting, severe belly pain, black or bloody stools, a high fever, or signs of dehydration deserve a call right away.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
