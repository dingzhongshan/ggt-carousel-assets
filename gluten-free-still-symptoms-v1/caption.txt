You cut the bread. You read the labels. And you still feel off.

That's frustrating. It's also more common than you'd think. About 1 in 5 people with celiac disease have symptoms that keep going or come back, even on a gluten-free diet.

Here's my take: that's a clue, not a failure.

NIH points to three usual suspects. Small amounts of gluten still sneaking in. Another health problem that looks similar. Or, rarely, refractory celiac disease.

And gluten hides well. Malt flavoring. Modified food starch. Lip balm. Toothpaste.

A food journal that includes your products, plus a chat with your doctor or a dietitian, can help you find the gap.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
