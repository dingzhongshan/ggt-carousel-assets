Hemorrhoids are one of those things nobody wants to bring up. So let's just talk about it.

NIDDK says hemorrhoids, also called piles, are swollen veins around the anus or in the lower rectum. They're common. About half of adults older than 50 have them.

Straining, long sits on the toilet, chronic constipation or diarrhea, and a low-fiber diet all make them more likely.

The gentle habits NIDDK lists: don't strain, don't linger on the toilet, eat more high-fiber foods, and drink water so the fiber works better. A warm sitz bath can help ease pain.

See a doctor if you notice any bleeding from your rectum, or if symptoms last after a week of home care.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
