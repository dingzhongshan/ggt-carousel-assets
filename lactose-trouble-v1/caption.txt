"Dairy upsets my stomach. What is wrong with me?" Nothing about asking that makes you strange.

A recent review explains that lactose is the main carbohydrate in milk, and that the ability to digest it varies widely around the world. For many people, the enzyme that handles lactose declines after weaning, a genetically determined change called lactase non-persistence. The review notes that approximately two-thirds of adults worldwide have reduced lactase, and that reduced lactase does not always mean symptoms.

It also points out that the terms people use for this are often misunderstood, so it is worth asking a clinician what applies to you.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
