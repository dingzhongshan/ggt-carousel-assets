Feeling lost in a long list of foods to avoid? The low FODMAP diet is often misunderstood as something you do forever.

The National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases explains that a doctor may recommend it to reduce or avoid certain foods containing carbohydrates that are hard to digest, called FODMAPs. A doctor may suggest trying it for a few weeks to see if symptoms improve. If they do, the next step may be slowly adding foods back, since you may be able to eat some of them without IBS symptoms.

A doctor or dietitian can guide you through it.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
