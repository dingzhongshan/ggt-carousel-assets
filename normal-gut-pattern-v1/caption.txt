What's "normal" for your gut? It's a wider range than most of us think.

Mayo Clinic describes the typical range as three times a day to three times a week, and says it helps to know what is typical for you. So your own pattern matters more than anyone else's.

An easy habit: jot down a few notes. How often, how it felt, anything unusual. If changes last more than a few weeks, or don't ease with self-care, that's worth a visit. And things like blood in the stool, black stools or unexplained weight loss are worth a prompt call.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
