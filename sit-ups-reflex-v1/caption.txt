"Just do sit-ups and it will go away."

If you have heard that about your bloating, this one is for you.

For some people, a swollen belly is not only about gas or weak muscles. Clinicians describe a muscle reflex where the diaphragm tightens and moves down while the front belly wall relaxes and moves out. It can show up as a flat belly in the morning that grows through the day, and the amount of gas is often too small to explain it.

Not everyone with bloating has this, and only a qualified clinician can tell what is going on for you.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
