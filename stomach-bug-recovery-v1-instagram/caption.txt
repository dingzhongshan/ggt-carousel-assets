Got through a stomach bug but still don't feel like yourself? Totally normal.

Quick myth-buster first: "stomach flu" isn't the flu. It's a viral infection of your intestines, and flu viruses don't cause it.

NIDDK says most people recover on their own, usually within a week. Your main job is replacing the fluids and electrolytes you lose. For most adults, water, broth, juice, sports drinks and a few saltines can help.

A strict bland diet? Research says it doesn't help. When your appetite comes back, your usual meals can too. Milk may feel rough for a month or more, though.

See a doctor right away for signs of dehydration, bloody or black stools, a high fever, or diarrhea that lasts more than 2 days.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
