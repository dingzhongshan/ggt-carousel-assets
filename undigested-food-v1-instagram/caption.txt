Ever wonder why digestion leaves leftovers?

NIDDK lists undigested parts of food, fluid and older cells as waste products of digestion. Parts of food that are still too large become stool. Some foods also contain sugars, starches and fiber that your stomach and small intestine don't fully digest. Those carbs pass to your large intestine, where bacteria break them down and create gas.

Talk with your doctor if gas symptoms bother you, change suddenly, or come with belly pain, constipation, diarrhea or weight loss.

Gentle daily habits, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor.
